# 4 — Full reference list (100 songs)

**Reference window:** first Hot 100 top-10 weeks from the chart dated **2026-05-30** back to **2021-09-18**. That is the 100 most recent distinct rap songs, counting back from the chart dated 2026-11-21 (research cutoff 2026-11-22).

**Ordering rule:** by the chart week each song *first* reached positions 1–10, newest first. Ties within a week are sorted by position that week. Each song is counted once. Billboard's combined credits and remixes are followed, so Billboard's mid-run re-credit of "Carnival" from Kanye West to Ye counts as one song.

**Genre rule:** rap = Billboard placed the song on Hot Rap Songs or on a rap-only component chart (Rap Streaming Songs, Rap Airplay), or Billboard reporting says so. Hot R&B Songs and Hot Rap Songs are mutually exclusive halves of Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs, so R&B-classified songs by rappers are excluded and logged below.

**Rank and date source:** every weekly Hot 100 chart since 1958, from the public `mhollingshead/billboard-hot-100` mirror of billboard.com. It has no missing weeks since 2015. All 155 candidate dates and ranks were re-derived from the weekly charts, and Billboard or press reporting confirmed the rank in a sample of weeks (see `data/classification_evidence.csv`).

**Status: PROVISIONAL.** 4 entries are included without direct classification evidence: each was pre-tagged rap, and a targeted re-check found only indirect evidence (a Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs peak, which does not separate rap from R&B, or album-mates on rap charts). They are marked †. 1 entry is included because album-mates from the same release week were on a rap chart, with no page showing the title itself; marked ‡. 4 excluded candidates above the cut rest on inference or indirect evidence, not a direct listing. Any one of these could move the cut. See *4d. Boundary and open items*.

## 4b. Per-track measurements used in the findings

**Legend.**
- **Basis:** **S** = a cited source the verifier did not reject (or a later gap sweep cited its own); **U** = the researcher cited a source, but the independent verifier could not find support, so it is *not* counted as sourced; **R** = unverified recollection, never counted; **?** = unknown.
- **Runtime:** ✔ = verifier confirmed; ✎ = verifier corrected; ◦ = verifier could not confirm; ♪ = taken from a BPM-site listing.
- **Grid / felt BPM:** grid = DAW tempo convention, felt = rap-bar pulse. Every felt value is a genre-convention inference; no source describes the feel. Marks: ✔ = verifier agreed; ✎ = corrected; ◦ = verifier could not confirm; · = one listing or the tempo sweep only; ⚠ = conflicting listings (excluded from M-1); "unresolved" = half- or full-time cannot be told from listings (excluded from M-1).
- **Hook first** = the hook arrives before the first verse (a short spoken line, tag or sample intro may come before it).
- **Return change** = what a cited source says changes when a section comes back, coded by me (beat/bed, a new voice, the same rapper's register, a spoken insert); "U" = described, but the verifier rejected the matching claim; — = not documented.
- **Borrowing:** s = sample, i = interpolation, c = dialogue clip, r = self-reuse; (u) = uncredited. ✓ = confirmed or added by the verifier; U = verifier could not re-find it; ✗ = rejected and not counted; no mark = cited by research, not flagged. — = none documented (unknown, not a verified absence). Full credits are in `data/track_measurements.csv`.
- **2nd voice** = the Billboard credit names a second vocalist (producer-only credits such as Metro Boomin do not count).
- **Path:** **D** = debuted in the top 10; **C** = climbed in from the rank shown. wk = weeks in the top 10; tot = total Hot 100 weeks; AB = album-bomb cut (3+ songs by the same lead artist first entering the top 10 that week).
